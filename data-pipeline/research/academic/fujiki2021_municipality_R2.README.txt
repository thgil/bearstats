Coefficient of determination (R2) between observed and model-predicted autumn Asiatic black bear occurrence, per municipality (11 municipalities, Hyogo Prefecture, coded W1-W5/A/E1-E5 in the source), for 5 candidate GLMM models varying which Fagaceae mast-index species are included (3sp=beech+Q.crispula+Q.serrata; 2sp drops Qc; single-species Qc/Fc/Qs). Data period 2005-2018 (14 years). *P<0.05 **P<0.01 ***P<0.001. Source: Fujiki, D. 2021, 'A model to predict the occurrence of Asiatic black bears at the municipal level using mast production data', Ursus 32:article e6, Table 3 (BioOne, open bronze access, full text fetched).
